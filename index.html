Selected Projects

Featured · multi-agent workflow

SimpleNote: A Multi-Agent Workflow for Auditable Clinical Text Simplification

  • Python
  • LangGraph
  • LangChain
  • OpenAI API
  • UMLS
  • pytest
  • Built a ten-node LangGraph state graph that orchestrates sub-agents for fact extraction, terminology retrieval, planning, writing, and checking; every draft ends in an accept, retry, or reject decision with its reason saved.
  • Aligned source and output facts with hybrid embedding-plus-lexical scoring and Hungarian matching, so omissions and unsupported additions are caught fact by fact.
  • Cut simulated-reader questions per 100 words by 45.3% versus direct prompting on 18 MIMIC-III notes; 18/18 runs finished with zero runtime errors, backed by a pytest suite of 100+ tests.

Experience-Guided LLM Review [3]

  • LLM review agents
  • structured memory
  • human adjudication
  • Found that injected experience helps reviewers unevenly, and designed independent general and experience-guided reviewers whose complementary findings are kept after verification.
  • Raised error-detection recall from 49.80% to 61.67% over two combined general reviews in a 20-case human-adjudicated evaluation, doubling confirmed new detections (9 → 18).

Prompt Design and Reader Simulation for Patient-Facing Notes [1]

  • 2×3 controlled study
  • blinded LLM reader simulation
  • human fact annotation
  • Ran a controlled study of short versus detailed prompts and three revision strategies over 120 outputs, with blinded simulated readers and two human fact annotators.
  • Showed that better readability scores can hide more reader confusion: detailed prompts scored grade 9.3 versus 13.4, yet drew more simulated-reader questions per 100 words.

Neuro-Symbolic Consistency Verifier

  • PyTorch
  • first-order logic
  • ClinicalBERT
  • differentiable predicates
  • Trained an interpretable verifier that turns sentences into first-order-logic facts, aligns them, and learns predicate thresholds by backpropagation.
  • Built a 1,250-pair synthetic-error benchmark and reached 83% accuracy and 0.86 inconsistency F1 on held-out source sentences.

RAG over a Biomedical Knowledge Base [2]

  • SciSpaCy
  • UMLS
  • FAISS
  • GPT-4o
  • Co-developed a pipeline that links clinical terms to UMLS concepts and grounds generation in definitions retrieved by FAISS semantic search.
  • Raised completeness from 72.7% to 77.6% and cut errors from 6 to 2 on 20 MIMIC-III notes.

MedShapley: Layer Attribution and Selective LoRA for Llama-3-8B

  • PyTorch
  • PEFT/LoRA
  • Shapley attribution
  • Designed matched-control Shapley attribution over all 32 layers to separate layers that lean toward factuality from those that lean toward readability.
  • Fine-tuned Llama-3-8B-Instruct with LoRA under attribution-guided freezing, uniform adaptation, and three random-freeze controls.
